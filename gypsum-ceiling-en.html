Gypsum Ceilings and Hidden Lighting: Details That Change a Room

How to choose the right ceiling design and lighting for your Riyadh living room.

Why gypsum board?

Gypsum board is a practical way to hide wiring and level a ceiling without major structural work. It also allows layered lighting that adds depth and a sense of space.

Lighting types

Perimeter hidden lighting gives a soft glow, spot lights highlight details, and a chandelier becomes the focal point. Using them together creates balance.

Tips before installation

Decide AC and detector locations before installation, leave access panels for pipes, and use moisture-resistant boards in bathrooms and kitchens.
