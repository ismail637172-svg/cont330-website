How to Choose a Reliable Renovation Contractor in Riyadh

A simple checklist that protects you from delays and hidden extra costs.

Ask for previous work

Before-and-after photos of real projects show the level of workmanship and attention to detail.

Get a written quote

A clear quote lists items, quantities and duration. Missing details are the first sign of later extras.

Agree on a timeline

Set start and delivery dates and a daily follow-up method so you know what happened each day.

Warranty after delivery

A good contractor gives a written warranty and comes back to fix any notes after handover.
