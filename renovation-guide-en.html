Home Renovation in Riyadh: Where to Start

Practical steps before any renovation: inspection, finding the real cause, a clear quote and execution.

Why diagnosis comes first

Before any demolition, find the real cause. Bathroom moisture or wall cracks often come from a hidden leak or thermal expansion. A proper inspection saves you the cost of redoing the work.

Steps of a successful renovation

First: inspect and document the site. Second: identify and write down the real causes. Third: issue a detailed quote with items and timeline. Fourth: execute in phases with daily follow-up.

How long does it take?

Small jobs like painting a room or fixing a bathroom usually take a few days. A full villa fit-out may take several weeks depending on the area and structural work.

How to choose a contractor

Ask for previous work, request a written contract, and confirm a clear warranty. A good contractor explains the problem in simple words before asking you to sign.
